Privacy Policy

Ducks in Hiding, a game by Sneaky Duck Studio · Effective 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Ducks in Hiding uses, who receives it and what choices you have. In short: we do not run any servers that collect information about you, and the game needs no account. Ads and purchases are handled by Google, which collects some information to do that.

Information kept on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone or tablet: the levels you cleared, stars, best times, hints, second chances, duck outfits, your daily-hunt streak and the name you type for the leaderboard. This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us, and it is erased when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's advertising software in the game collects and uses:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which also indicates your approximate location (for example your city or region);
  • how you interact with the ads and the game, such as ad views and taps;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the advertising software.

Google may use this information to show ads based on your interests. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices: in your Android settings (Privacy, then Ads) you can reset or delete your advertising ID, which turns off interest-based ads. You can also buy "No ads" in the game's shop to remove the ads shown between levels.

Purchases

Purchases in the game's shop are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. Google tells the game which items you bought so that it can give them to you and restore them after a reinstall.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Children

Ducks in Hiding is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Keeping and deleting information

We hold no personal information about you on our own systems, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove the information kept on your device, uninstall the game or clear its data in your Android settings. For information held by Google, use the controls in your Google account and the advertising settings described above.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches you.
